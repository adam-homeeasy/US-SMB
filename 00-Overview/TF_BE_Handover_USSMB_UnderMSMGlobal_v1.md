### Classification (S0.2, draft, needs founder confirmation)

- **Category and vertical:** Service business, B2B/B2C hybrid leaning B2B-in-practice. Sells to SMB owners (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, med spas, dental, law firms) as a productized digital-growth system, delivered largely programmatically. Closest existing category-adaptive heuristics block in the engine: **Service business (EXTRAPOLATED, unevidenced)**, with a real secondary pull toward the **Technology/SaaS** block given the programmatic, tool-built delivery model. **This will be the first live engagement testing either category-adaptive block.** Log back what actually worked once it runs.
- **Operating stage:** Pre-launch as a branded offering, but not pre-launch as a business. The delivery capability (SEO/social/YouTube audits, programmatic build tooling) is already built and tested; the sales motion (300 targeted accounts/month, diagnostic-led outreach) is planned but not yet run at volume per the handover doc. Read this as "early-operating," not "pre-launch idea": there is a real product, a real price ladder, a real target list methodology, just not yet a track record under a locked brand name.
- **What already carries real equity, and this is the load-bearing fact for the whole engagement:** MSM Global itself. It is not a blank parent. Per its v4 brand guidelines: an established positioning ("Growth Systems for Growing Businesses"), a five-dimension personality (Experienced Not Academic, Direct Not Aggressive, Systematic Not Rigid, Accessible Not Informal, Proven Not Promotional), a full visual system (Deep Navy #0D1B2A / Amber Gold #D4A017 / Brand Navy #1F3864, League Spartan + Montserrat + Inter typography, consulting-grade visual language explicitly inspired by Bain, McKinsey, Deloitte, EY, Accenture), and, most importantly, **two existing flagship sub-brands already running the exact pattern this engagement needs to repeat**: VaultIQ™ (Revenue Recovery Methodology) and Leasify™ (Leasing Operations System for US residential operators). Both are single coined or suggestive words, capitalized, trademarked, each with its own dedicated section and a light color differentiation inside MSM's system (VaultIQ gets Brand Navy, Leasify gets Light background) rather than a fully separate visual identity.

### S0.4 Contradiction check, one real flag before G0.0

Something worth surfacing explicitly rather than letting it pass silently, because it's a genuine tension, not a settled fact: MSM Global's brand voice is built around "No hype," "Results-first, Analytical, Executive," case studies quoting things like "$1M+ GMV managed" and "EBITDA-positive in 11 months," aimed at businesses that can weigh a senior-operator-equivalent hire. The US SMB Digital Growth plan is a **$199-entry, high-volume, aggressively-priced local-services play**, explicitly *not* meant to compete on custom agency depth, targeting HVAC and plumbing and dental owners who decide fast on comparatively small numbers. Both things can be true and coherent (VaultIQ and Leasify already prove MSM can hold more than one price point and audience under one house), but the register gap between "$1M+ GMV managed, EBITDA-positive" and "$199 website, $999 full system" is wider than the gap between VaultIQ and Leasify's existing audiences. Whether this new brand needs a more pronounced accent inside MSM's system than VaultIQ or Leasify got, or whether the diagnostic-led "we found $X of leakage in your business" framing is exactly what carries MSM's executive register down to a $199 price point without cheapening it, is a real S1B/G0.1 decision, not something to assume either way going in.

## 2. S1B guidance: vehicle and architecture, largely pre-answered by precedent

Per Module 00's Succession Test and architecture pattern map: this is an **Institution** (separable from any one founder, matches MSM's own separable-institution pattern), and the architecture pattern is almost certainly **Endorsed sub-brand within an existing portfolio**, the same pattern VaultIQ and Leasify already occupy, not a from-scratch standalone brand competing with MSM Global. Portfolio position: **beneath MSM Global**, as its third named flagship offering. Confirm this at S1B rather than skip it: the naming pilot's own lesson (recorded in Module 00) is that an unasked vehicle question costs hours of territory work built on the wrong premise, and here the answer is unusually well-evidenced by two real precedents, which is worth stating explicitly at G0.1 rather than assumed.

Practical consequence for S3 (Taste Board) and S4 (Territory Mapping): VaultIQ and Leasify are mandatory taste board entries per the standing rule that a founder's own existing names are the strongest taste evidence available. Decode their construction (single word, suggestive-to-fanciful, function-adjacent, trademarkable, ends clean for a ™) as the register this name needs to match or deliberately extend.
