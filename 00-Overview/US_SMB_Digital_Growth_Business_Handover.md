# US SMB Digital Growth Systems Business --- Handover

## 1. Executive Summary

The business sells **low-cost, productized digital growth infrastructure
to US SMBs**, using a highly programmatic delivery model.

The core thesis is simple:

-   US SMBs often have fragmented digital sales infrastructure: weak
    websites, poor lead capture, disconnected funnels, slow follow-up,
    limited CRM discipline, and repetitive work that can be automated.
-   Conventional US agencies frequently charge thousands of dollars for
    website, funnel, CRM, and automation work.
-   The business can deliver much of this work programmatically using
    internally built tools, Claude Code, low-cost API tokens, reusable
    components, and founder-led implementation.
-   This creates room to enter the market at unusually aggressive prices
    while retaining attractive contribution margins.
-   Sales initially remain founder-led: two founders, including one
    partner able to work US hours.
-   Outreach infrastructure is also largely in-house. Primary variable
    acquisition costs are email infrastructure plus calling/texting; an
    American number is approximately \$20.
-   The initial objective is **validation and cash generation**, not
    maximizing price.

The model should be treated as a **productized implementation business
evolving toward software-like delivery**, not a conventional custom
web/AI agency.

## 2. Target Customer

### Primary Market

US small and medium businesses where:

-   each inbound lead has meaningful economic value;
-   phone calls, forms, appointments, or consultations drive revenue;
-   response speed affects conversion;
-   missed calls and weak follow-up create measurable leakage;
-   the owner can make relatively fast purchasing decisions;
-   one or a few incremental customers can economically justify a \$999
    system.

### Initial Vertical Candidates

Priority verticals include:

-   HVAC
-   Plumbing
-   Roofing
-   Med spas
-   Dental practices
-   Law firms
-   Other high-value local service businesses

The first tests should **not target all SMB categories simultaneously**.

Initial approach:

**2 verticals × 150 accounts each = 300 targeted businesses/month.**

Month 1 identifies which vertical, offer, diagnostic, and message
performs better. Month 2 should reallocate volume toward the winner
rather than mechanically repeating the Month 1 split.

## 3. Core Positioning

Do not position the company primarily as:

> Cheap website development

or:

> An AI automation agency

The stronger proposition is:

> **We identify where your digital sales system is losing leads and
> revenue, then build the website, funnel, CRM and automation needed to
> fix it.**

This moves the sales conversation from **cost of development** to
**economic leakage and return**.

The \$199 website is therefore an acquisition product and downsell,
while the desired commercial outcome is the \$999 complete system.

## 9. Sales Strategy

### Fundamental Sales Principle

Do not lead with:

> We build websites for \$199.

Lead with a specific problem discovered in the prospect's business.

Conceptually:

> We analysed your digital sales setup and found several issues. Three

Because cash labour cost is currently negligible, it is easy to
overestimate profitability.

Track:

**Founder sales minutes/customer\
Founder build minutes/customer\
Support minutes/customer/month**

## 22. Delivery Philosophy

The structural advantage should **not** be defined as:

> Indian labour is cheaper.

The stronger advantage is:

> **Prospecting → diagnosis → proposal → build → deployment is
> increasingly programmatic.**

That creates a more durable cost advantage than simply using lower-cost
labour.

Conventional agencies may charge \$2,000--\$5,000+ partly because every
account consumes substantial human design, development,
account-management, and sales time.

The business should systematically eliminate those labour requirements.

## 25. Decision Framework After 600 Accounts

### Weak Result

Indicators:

-   low engagement;
-   few demos;
-   low demo conversion;
-   predominantly \$199 sales;
-   high delivery/support time.

Action:

**Do not scale volume. Fix vertical, diagnostic, offer, messaging, or
product.**

### Promising Result

Indicators:

-   meaningful demo flow;
-   3%+ account-to-\$999 conversion;
-   healthy downsell revenue;
-   manageable founder time;
-   recurring adoption.

Action:

**Increase outreach while improving automation.**

### Strong Result

Indicators:

-   5%+ account-to-\$999 conversion;
-   high RPTA;
-   low human delivery time;
-   strong \$99 attachment/retention;
-   one vertical materially outperforming.

Action:

**Concentrate on winning vertical and scale outbound aggressively.**

## 28. Core Business Hypothesis

The model does not require premium agency pricing to work.

It relies on four structural advantages:

**1. Very low acquisition infrastructure cost**

In-house email outreach, inexpensive calling/texting, founder-led sales.

**2. Programmatic diagnosis**

Prospects can be approached with specific intelligence rather than
generic pitches.

**3. Programmatic production**

Websites, funnels, audits and automations can be produced with
substantially less labour than conventional agency delivery.

**4. Recurring monetization**

The \$999 implementation creates upfront cash while the \$99/month
system creates a recurring revenue base.

The initial downside thesis is deliberately modest:

> **If 8% of 300 targeted businesses buy only a \$199 website, monthly
> revenue is approximately \$4,776.**

The upside thesis is that diagnostics and outcome-led selling move a
portion of those customers into the **\$999 + \$99/month Complete Growth
System**, materially increasing both immediate revenue and recurring
revenue.

## 29. North-Star Objective

The business should not optimize for:

-   websites built;
-   audits generated;
-   emails sent;
-   raw customer count.

The long-term objective is:

> **Build a repeatable system that identifies revenue leakage in US
> SMBs, sells a standardized solution to fix it, deploys that solution
> programmatically, and generates recurring revenue with minimal human
> intervention.**

The key operating equation is:

**Targeted Accounts × Paid Conversion × Revenue per Customer − Variable
Acquisition/Delivery Cost**

During the first two months, the most useful summary metric is:

> **Revenue per Targeted Account, accompanied by Human Minutes per
> Customer.**

Together, those two numbers will determine whether the model should
remain a small founder-led cash-generating business or be scaled into a
much larger productized SMB growth platform.
