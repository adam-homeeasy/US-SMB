## Where the line reports are wrong or weak

1. **03-REVLABS-FIT.md, plan.** "Starter $97 until a 4th sub-account." With a shared spine, day one needs RevLabs sales, RevLabs Texas, HomeEasy, TF internal and the sandbox from 01: 5 against Starter's 3 [ghl-pricing-page, via 01]. Fix: Unlimited from the day HomeEasy's build starts. Starter only while RevLabs is the sole user.
2. **03, missed-call text back** (workflow 13 and the $999 table). It prices a feature the locked rule forbids as if it works. Fix: take it out of the $999 until Adam scopes the rule and a lawyer reviews (R5).
3. **03, $999 treated as one-off** with a $99 care fee the context never locks. With no fee, each Full-system sub-account costs $46 to $55 a month for ever, and the $100 credit covers usage for only about 6.7 months. Fix: $999 setup plus a required care fee of at least $139 (03's own number), set before the first proposal.
4. **03, Texas clients.** The Texas wall covers our prospecting only. A Texas client on Growth or Full would text Texas consumers. Fix: email-only automations for Texas clients until counsel (R11).
5. **03, audit cost.** "Weekly screenshot audit" and "1 hour per client" are in the text but not in any cost table. Fix: add 0.25 to 1 hour per sub-account per month.
6. **03, cold email on LC Email.** Shared pool and unknown policy put the other two lines at risk. Fix: own SMTP or a cold-email tool (R18).
7. **03, "US entity: not now".** It fixes MSM's A2P brand, SaaS mode, TF's Connect and the wallet card. Fix: start it now for TF. RevLabs gets it free.
8. **02-LEASIFY-FIT.md, 12 SMS segments per lead.** 6 AI replies per lead means 6 out and about 6 in: 12 before the first-touch text, 3 tour reminders, no-show texts and reactivation. AI replies often pass 160 characters, so 2 segments each. 20 to 30 is more likely [assumption]. At 20, SMS is $230 not $138, and base cost at 10 clients is $365.78 (margin $133, 27% at $499). 9. **02, service labour excluded.** The retainer pays people to hand off tours, chase applications and review the AI. 10. **02, listing-site leads.** Row 2 prices an SMS in 60 seconds while section 5 defers opt-in to counsel. Design and cost assume the answer is yes. Fix: email or call first as the default, SMS as the upgrade (R2).
11. **02, Texas ch. 302 is not mentioned** though Leasify texts Texas renters. Fix: counsel, reply-only texting in Texas until then.
12. **02, SLA guarantee** is exposed to one GHL outage across all clients (R9). Fix: SLA wording.

## What breaks at 100 clients

- **A2P:** 100 registrations per line, each 1 to 7 business days with rejections. $150 to $1,000 a month in campaign fees per line [ghl-docs, via 01]. Needs one owner and a queue.
- **Snapshots:** 100 sub-accounts on different versions, no version control [via 01]. One bad push hits many clients.
- **Audits:** about 25 hours a week per 100 sub-accounts at 15 minutes each, weekly.
- **Add-ons:** Leasify alone pays about $1,000 a month in Workflow Pro and $1,000 in A2P campaign fees.
- **Float:** about $17,000 a month of Leasify usage prepaid through the agency wallet.
- **Support:** GHL terms make us the only help desk [ghl-docs, via 01]. Failed texts and missed bookings land on a team working IST nights.
- **Email:** shared LC Email pool, one careless sub-account hurts others. Dedicated IP is $59 each [ghl-docs, via 01].
- **Secrets:** 100+ Private Integration Tokens to store and rotate [assumption, via 04]. One broken token silently drops a client from the founder report.
- **Outage:** one GHL incident hits every sub-account on all three lines at once.
- **Opt-outs:** thousands of STOPs a month to reconcile across two databases.
- **Exit:** 200 to 400 hours per line at 2 to 4 hours a client, plus 100 A2P re-registrations and number ports.

## Kill criteria

1. **Lock-offs fail.** Live check shows power dialer, voicemail drop or recording cannot be disabled or hidden per sub-account or role, and cannot be read by API: stop GHL for RevLabs client delivery. Founder-only prospecting stays.
2. **Consent breach.** The nightly check finds SMS sent without a logged consent flag twice in 90 days: SMS off in that line until the gate is rebuilt. A third time: no GHL SMS for that line.
3. **Carrier or GHL warning.** Any carrier block, A2P campaign suspension or GHL abuse warning: pause the cause that day. A second in 90 days: move that line to its own agency account or off GHL.
4. **Leasify misses its own promise.** HomeEasy after 30 days live on GHL: median first response over 5 minutes, or 2 or more GHL-caused SLA misses in a month. Stop Leasify on GHL, keep Amy Scott's current setup, message through Twilio plus Postgres.
5. **Sync fails.** An opt-out in GHL missing from Postgres or Supabase for over 24 hours, twice: GHL becomes send-only behind the Postgres gate, or is dropped for that line.
6. **Usage eats the retainer.** Median Leasify client usage above 40% of retainer after lead caps: move bulk SMS to direct Twilio or reprice.
7. **Team cannot run it.** By day 60 the GHL owner cannot provision a compliant sub-account from the snapshot in under 3 hours: cut GHL to RevLabs prospecting only.
8. **Vendor turns.** Suspension notice, a reseller-terms change that touches us, or a price rise over 25% on plan or LC usage: start the exit runbook.

## Challenges to the likely verdict

1. **Buying GHL at all is overkill for 180 days.** RevLabs needs a CRM, click-to-call and an email sender. HomeEasy already has Amy Scott. Case against: $297 plus 6 to 8 weeks of learning for three lines that each use a slice.
   - **My call: buy, but late.** RevLabs' use is settled and fits Starter. Leasify needs inbox, calendars, reminders and an A2P flow that would take longer to build. Run the live check in the free trial first. Pay for Unlimited only when HomeEasy's build starts.
2. **A lighter tool is enough for Leasify.** Twilio plus Postgres plus a booking tool, since Postgres and Python already hold the hard logic.
   - **My call: GHL still wins on time to the first paid client.** But GHL sits behind the Postgres gate, not the other way round, and Amy Scott stays where she is until a side-by-side test on HomeEasy. If kill criterion 4 hits, the lighter stack is close because Postgres was the record all along.
3. **A lighter tool is enough for TF.** 04 says a sheet works for 2 to 3 prospects and GHL is only worth it if bought anyway.
   - **My call: change TF to "Don't use GHL for now".** Sheet plus email until 30 Nov and 10 or more live prospects. Sovit's hours go to the pilot and Stripe Connect, not to learning GHL. Re-test after 30 Nov.
4. **"Shared spine" is the wrong frame.** The lines share no buyer, brand, data or workflow. The only shared thing is one bill, and sharing it creates one blast radius (R7) and one login brand (R28). Separate accounts at scale cost about $394 a month more (Leasify Unlimited, RevLabs Unlimited, TF Starter).
   - **My call: one agency now.** One operator and one learning curve matter more than blast radius at 0 to 10 clients. Move Leasify to its own agency at 10 paying clients or at the first compliance warning.
5. **The US entity is "not now" in 03 and a flag in 04.** It unblocks MSM's A2P brand, SaaS mode, TF's Stripe Connect and a wallet card that avoids 3DS.
   - **My call: start it now with a CA.** It is on TF's critical path whatever happens to GHL.
6. **Three "use for part" verdicts came from agents each told the plan is split three ways.** Each line looks cheap because the others pay.
   - **My call: RevLabs and Leasify pass on their own.** One Leasify client at $499 or four RevLabs Growth clients covers the whole $297 [via 02, 03]. TF does not pass.
