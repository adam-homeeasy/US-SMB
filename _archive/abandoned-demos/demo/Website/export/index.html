Know the warning signs

4 furnace problems worth a call

No heat

Pilot won't stay lit, or the blower runs but the air stays cold.

Strange noises

Banging, squealing, or rattling on startup usually means a loose or failing part.

Thermostat problems

Settings don't match the room, or the system won't respond to changes.

Poor airflow

Weak air from vents, uneven room temperatures, or a system that runs constantly.
