What happens next

The site is only the first piece. Here's what each next phase actually does, in the order it tends to matter most.

1
Product: Review

Review

Your 4.7 star rating is a real strength. The gap is visibility and response consistency, since only 15% of your reviews have ever gotten a reply. Review picks up monitoring immediately: it drafts owner responses for your approval, and tracks the trend the local map pack actually rewards.

  • A drafted response to every new review. Nothing posts without your sign-off.
  • A monthly rating and review-count trend, checked against your three closest competitors.
  • A simple link or QR code you can hand customers so leaving a review takes them ten seconds.
2
Product: Traffic Optimizer

Traffic Optimizer

Your site launched with the schema and NAP fixes already built in. This is what keeps that working instead of quietly going stale.

  • A content refresh every quarter, since pages left untouched lose AI answer engine citations fast.
  • Ongoing brand mentions built on the review and forum platforms your category actually gets discussed on.
  • A recheck of your map pack ranking for real service searches, not just people already searching your name.
3
Product: Lead Management

Lead Management

Two of your three closest competitors already take bookings online. You don't yet. This adds a real CRM and an AI front desk on top of the site that's already live: it answers after hours calls and texts, books straightforward jobs straight into your calendar, and only hands you the ones that need a real conversation.

  • Online booking and a maintenance-plan signup path, so not every visitor has to call.
  • A seasonal lead magnet, like a free pre-winter furnace check checklist, to capture people who aren't ready to book yet.
  • After-hours call and text handling, with a human routed in for anything urgent or unusual.
4
Product: Invoicing

Invoicing

Chasing payment eats hours most owners never track. This connects to your bookkeeping and drafts every overdue reminder for you, gentle for good payers and firmer for repeat late ones, with a plain monthly summary of what's actually coming in.

  • Draft, tone-matched payment reminders for every overdue invoice.
  • A monthly cash position summary you can read in under a minute.
  • Nothing sent or reconciled without a human checking it first.
5
Product: CRM

CRM

One place for every contact, job, and past conversation, instead of a phone full of texts and a notebook by the register. This is what Lead Management and Invoicing both plug into underneath.

  • Every customer and job history in one searchable place.
  • A simple pipeline view of who's booked, who's quoted, and who's gone quiet.
  • Works standalone, or as the shared base for the other products above.
